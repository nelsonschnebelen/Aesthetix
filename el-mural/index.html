The Setup

The biggest margarita day of the year landed on a Monday.

In 2025, Cinco de Mayo fell on Monday, May 5 — historically one of the quietest shifts of the week. Most restaurants fight for attention with a post on the day itself, when everyone has already decided where they’re going.

Dineline’s bet for El Mural was the opposite: the money is decided in the three weeks before the holiday, not on it. So the entire push — budget, offers, booking campaigns — ran from April 14 through the holiday week, reaching people while they were still choosing.

By Monday afternoon, the dining room was full. The proof is in the data: Cinco day itself looked like any other day in the ad account — because the work was already done.
The 2025 Playbook

Three weeks of build. Two channels. One job each.

From April 14 to May 6, 2025, El Mural ran $1,368 across Google and Meta — roughly 110,000 impressions and 4,015 clicks — each channel doing what it does best.
