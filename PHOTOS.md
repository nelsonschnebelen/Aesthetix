## Notes

- Any format is fine (`.jpg`, `.png`, `.heic`, straight off a phone). Keep the
  filename and extension as `.jpg` if you can; if you upload a different
  extension, say so and I will convert and rename.
- Full resolution is fine — I resize and compress on the way in. The current
  set is ~2 MB total for 13 images.
- Shape is a preference, not a requirement. Anything gets cropped sensibly;
  the shapes above just mean less of the picture is thrown away.
- Cropping, colour and compression are free for me to do. Send the raw file.
